# Product Requirements Document: SpeakUp

**Status:** Draft / Active
**Date:** October 2026
**Author:** Product Team

## 1. TL;DR (The Problem)
**Who is this for?**
Indian students, job seekers, and aspiring founders who want to improve their verbal communication skills.

**What is the specific pain point?**
Many individuals struggle with spoken communication in professional and high-stakes environments (e.g., interviews, startup pitches, meetings) due to a lack of practice, confidence, and access to constructive, unbiased feedback.

**Evidence:**
There is a clear market need for an accessible, non-judgmental, and immediate communication coaching tool that can simulate real-world scenarios and provide actionable insights.

## 2. The Proposal
**High-level Solution:**
SpeakUp is an AI-powered communication coach tailored to the specific needs of Indian professionals. The core loop consists of:
`Scenario → User speaks → AI processes audio via STT → AI provides feedback → User is scored → User retries to improve`

**Core Experience & Features:**
- **Authentication & Onboarding:** Google OAuth & Email/Password (via Supabase), ensuring a seamless start.
- **Practice Arena:** Audio recording interface for answering prompts.
- **AI Coaching:** Real-time transcription powered by Groq Whisper STT, and actionable feedback generated by Groq LLaMA. Feedback is staged (highlighting 2-3 specific issues at a time) to avoid overwhelming the user.
- **Progress Tracking:** Score breakdowns, streaks, and attempt-by-attempt improvement tracking to visualize growth over time.
- **Curated Scenarios:** 30 seed scenarios in the database targeting relevant situations (e.g., job interviews, startup pitches).
- **Hindi-English Mode:** Specialized support for bilingual (Hinglish) speaking styles to match natural communication patterns.

## 3. Success Metrics
**Primary Metrics:**
- **Session Completion Rate:** The percentage of users who complete the entire core loop (Scenario -> Speak -> Feedback -> Score).
- **Retention (WAU/MAU):** Users returning to practice multiple times a week to build their streaks.

**Guardrail Metrics:**
- **STT & LLM Latency:** Audio processing and feedback generation must be fast (within a few seconds) to maintain a smooth user experience.
- **Error Rates:** Minimizing transcription errors and hallucinated feedback.

## 4. In/Out of Scope
**In Scope:**
- Next.js 14 App Router (TypeScript) frontend with a dark theme (#0A0A0A), mobile-first design, using Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui.
- Supabase backend (Database, Authentication, Storage).
- Groq Whisper (STT) and LLaMA (LLM) integrations via API.
- Session history with audio replay functionality.
- Daily challenges and real data visualization in the progress dashboard.
- Analytics integration (PostHog) and Email automation (Resend).

**Out of Scope:**
- Video recording or facial expression analysis (audio-only for MVP).
- Real-time live conversational AI avatars (system relies on an asynchronous prompt-respond-feedback loop for now).
- Monetization and Paywalls (focus is on initial user acquisition and product-market fit).

## 5. Open Questions
- **AI Token Limits & Costs:** How do we optimize prompts to manage Groq API costs at scale while maintaining high-quality, personalized feedback?
- **Data Privacy & Storage:** What is the retention policy for user audio files in Supabase Storage to balance cost and user privacy?
- **Bilingual Nuances:** How accurately will Whisper and LLaMA handle heavily mixed Hindi-English sentences out of the box, and what fine-tuning might be required?
